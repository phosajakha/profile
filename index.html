About

Jakha Phosa
(2016) • jakha. 001 / archive

Grain

[practice]
NEG. 04A JP

I started as a graphic designer, then fell in love with the camera while photographing my own design work for documentation. Now the two go hand in hand: design gives structure, photography gives soul.

My approach is simple — first understand what an image or layout is trying to say, then decide its visual form. The end result has to feel honest and easy to remember.

Beyond that, I enjoy documenting city streets and old print archives — a habit that always sneaks its way into how I build a layout.
